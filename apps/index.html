CREST · Actor Journeys

Four products, one record

The Actor Journeys reference, built as the separate applications it outlines, each shipped as its own deployment the way its product would be — all reading and writing the same live CREST services. Nothing on any screen is fixture data held in the browser.

Worker · W-1

The worker's app

The wallet, not a dashboard — my record, my money and why any of it is held, who checked me, what I agreed to.
Field · W-2 / W-4

The field app

Registering a worker who cannot self-register; voice consent read aloud; confirm what you saw; close the roster.
Console · G / P / F

The console

One console, role-based views — instance, organisation, project, defining the work, payment set-up, the custodian's queues.
Verifier · V-1 / V-2

Verification

Account-free checking: yes, plus facts, nothing identifying. Refusals shown as refusals. Bounded batches.
